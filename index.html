Difficultly Modifiers

Here are ways you can adjust the game to match the players abilities.

Normal

For normal difficulty, play as written on the instruction guide.

Easy

Changing suit can be said in English without penalty. Saying an Anishinaabemowin word wrong will not be penalized.

Easier

Changing suit can be said in English without penalty. Saying an Anishinaabemowin word wrong will not be penalized. Agindaasonan count goes up to 5 instead of 9.

Hard

All action cards must be said in Anishinaabemowin.

If you forget to say it (or forget the Anishinaabemowin word), another player can call it out by saying "Odaapin bezhig", and you must pick up a card. The call out must happen before the next player starters their turn.

Harder

All action cards must be said in Anishinaabemowin. If you take too long to say an Anishinaabemowin word, another player can call it out. The group decides if it was right to call it out. Majority rules. Flip a coin for a tie-breaker

Change suit

Agindaasonan

Counting

Uh g in daa so nun

Counting (inanimate)

The person who played leads the count in Anishinaabemowin (Ojibwe) to 9. Each player takes a turn saying a number until they reach 9 as a group.

The first person to say a number wrong, draws a card and the count ends.

If the count is successful, the leader picks a player to pick up 2 cards.

The game continues as normal and the suit returns to what was last played.

Playable on any colour.

Azheshkaan

Go back

Uh zhesh kaan

The play direction is reversed. Everyone says a number(1-9) as fast as they can. Last person to say a number draws a card. The turn ends. Suit returns to what was last played.

Playable on any colour.
